Journal Tier & Q1 Analysis期刊等级与Q1分析

app/pages/2_Journal_Tier.py — what share of output sits in Q1 journals, and how does that vary. app/pages/2_Journal_Tier.py —— 多少产出落在Q1期刊里， 这个比例又是怎么变化的。

What's on the page页面上有什么

  • Q1 share by university (bar) and by year (line), MAIN_YEAR_RANGE-scoped.按大学（柱状图）和按年份（折线图，限定在 MAIN_YEAR_RANGE 内）的Q1份额。
  • Citation performance across all four CiteScore quartiles (Q1–Q4) — mean FWCI, highly-cited share, uncited share, side by side.CiteScore四个分位（Q1–Q4）的引用表现对比——平均FWCI、高被引占比、零引用占比并排展示。
  • Q1-minus-non-Q1 FWCI gap, computed separately per field — is the advantage the same everywhere, or concentrated?按学科分别计算的Q1减非Q1的FWCI差距——这个优势是到处都一样，还是集中在少数学科？
  • Over/under-performing journals within their own tier — per-source mean FWCI vs. its quartile average, floor-filtered by publication count, top/bottom 12 in two tabs.在自己所在档位内跑赢/跑输的期刊——按来源的平均FWCI对比该分位的均值， 按发文量设下限过滤，前12/后12分两个标签页展示。
  • CiteScore percentile histogram (the right-skew that first confirmed the percentile direction), and a live CiteScore-vs-SJR Q1 agreement check.CiteScore百分位直方图（最初正是这个右偏形态确认了百分位的方向），以及一个实时算出的 CiteScore口径与SJR口径Q1吻合率检验。

Sub-question coverage子问题覆盖情况

✓ Do Q1 publications receive more citations than Q2, Q3 or Q4?Q1期刊的论文引用是否比Q2、Q3、Q4更高？ Data: the quartile_df table behind get_impact_by_citescore_quartile() — 4 rows (Q1/Q2/Q3/Q4, from citescore_quartile() bucketing the CiteScore percentile column) × the mean_fwci column, one bar per row. The binary Q1 flag used elsewhere on this page collapses Q2/Q3/Q4 into one "not Q1" bucket — this chart re-breaks the same CiteScore-percentile column into all four tiers, putting all four bars on one axis (position/length, the most accurately-judged channel) so the comparison the question actually asks for is direct, not inferred from a two-category flag.用的数据：get_impact_by_citescore_quartile() 生成的 quartile_df 表格——4行（Q1/Q2/Q3/Q4，由 citescore_quartile() 把CiteScore percentile列分桶得到），对比mean_fwci这一列， 一行一根柱子。这一页其它地方用的二元Q1标记会把Q2/Q3/Q4全部合并成"非Q1"一个桶——这张图把同一个 CiteScore百分位列重新拆成完整的四档，四根柱子放在同一根轴上（位置/长度是人眼最容易准确 判断的视觉通道），这样问题要的对比就是直接看出来的，不用从一个二分类标记里去推断。
✓ How large is the citation difference between journal tiers?各档位之间的引用差距有多大？ Same quartile_df table, read as a magnitude rather than a ranking: mean_fwci at row Q1 minus mean_fwci at row Q4 is the actual gap size — the text underneath states it as a number (mean_fwci, top_decile_share, and uncited_share all falling monotonically Q1→Q4) rather than leaving "large" for the reader to eyeball off the chart alone.同一张quartile_df表格，但读的是差距大小而不是排名：Q1行的mean_fwci 减去Q4行的mean_fwci就是实际差距，下方文字直接给出具体数字 （mean_fwci、top_decile_share、uncited_share三列 都在Q1→Q4之间单调下降），而不是把"有多大"这个问题留给读者自己在图上目测。
→ Which faculties/fields have the highest success rates in Q1 publishing?哪些院系/学科的Q1成功率最高？ This is a field-level ranking question, not a tier-level one — it belongs to the Field Analysis page's field_summary table's q1_share column (also charted as its own bar), sorted by exactly this number, rather than being duplicated here.这是一个学科层面的排名问题，不是档位层面的——它属于Field Analysis页面 field_summary 表格的q1_share列（也单独画了一张条形图）， 按这个数字排序，所以没有在这里重复。
✓ Is the Q1 citation advantage consistent across disciplines, or concentrated in a few?Q1的优势在各学科之间是否一致，还是集中在少数学科？ Data: the table from q1_advantage_by_field() — one row per field, a gap column defined as that field's Q1-publication mean_fwci minus its non-Q1 mean_fwci, computed separately per field rather than once on the pooled dataset — which is what actually tests "consistent" rather than assuming it: a single pooled gap can't distinguish one field driving the whole effect from every field sharing it equally, but a bar per field can and does (every row's gap is positive here, though the size varies several-fold).用的数据：q1_advantage_by_field() 输出的表格——每个学科一行， gap 列 = 该学科Q1论文的mean_fwci减去非Q1论文的mean_fwci， 在每个学科内部分别计算——而不是只在合并数据上算一次——这才是真正 检验"一致"这件事，而不是想当然地假设它成立：一个合并算出来的差距分不清是某一个学科 在拉动整体效应，还是所有学科都均匀共享这个效应，但按学科分开的柱状图能分清（这里每一行的 gap都是正的，只是大小相差好几倍）。
✓ Compare highly-cited (top-decile) publication rates across quartiles.对比各分位的高被引论文占比。 The same quartile_df table's top_decile_share column, plotted as the "Highly cited" bars sharing the same four-tier x-axis as the mean_fwci bars above them — a direct visual comparison, tier by tier.同一张quartile_df表格的top_decile_share列，画成"高被引" 柱子，跟上面mean_fwci柱状图共用同一个四档x轴——按档位逐一直接比较。
✓ Compare uncited-publication rates across quartiles.对比各分位的零引用论文占比。 Same table's uncited_share column, the mirror-image "Uncited" bars in the same paired chart — deliberately placed next to, not instead of, the highly-cited bars, since a tier can look strong on one measure and weak on the other.同一张表格的uncited_share列，是配对图里镜像的"零引用"柱子——特意放在 "高被引"柱子旁边而不是取代它，因为一个档位可能在其中一项指标上表现好、另一项却不好。
✓ Does publishing in Q1 increase the probability of becoming highly cited?发表在Q1是否会提高成为高被引论文的概率？ Answered by the shape of the same quartile_df table across all three of its columns (mean_fwci, top_decile_share, uncited_share), not a single Q1-vs-rest step: all three fall monotonically Q1→Q2→Q3→Q4, which is what rules out "Q1 is a cliff-edge, Q2/Q3/Q4 are all the same" and supports "yes, and it's a gradient that keeps going."靠的是同一张quartile_df表格三列（mean_fwci、 top_decile_share、uncited_share）的整体形状，而不是一次Q1对其它的 简单对比：三列在全部四档之间都是Q1→Q2→Q3→Q4单调下降，这正好排除了"Q1是断崖式的， Q2/Q3/Q4其实都差不多"这种可能，支持"是的，而且这是一条持续延伸的梯度"这个结论。
→ Which fields have the highest / improving / declining Q1 share?哪些学科的Q1份额最高/上升最快/下降最快？ Field Analysis page's field_summary.q1_share column for the "highest" half, and its separate growth_df.growth column (field_growth()'s period_growth per field) for the "improving/declining" half — again a field-level ranking, not something this tier-focused page computes.Field Analysis页面field_summary表格的q1_share列对应 "最高"这一半，独立的growth_df表格的growth列（field_growth() 按学科算的period_growth）对应"上升/下降最快"这一半——同样是 学科层面的排名，不是这个聚焦档位的页面该算的东西。
✓ Are there journals/publications within a tier that receive more citations than expected for that tier?同一档位内是否存在引用表现超出预期的期刊/论文？ Data: the sources table from get_overperforming_sources() — one row per (quartile, Scopus source title) pair, with publications, mean_fwci, and a gap column = that source's own mean_fwci minus its own quartile's mean_fwci, restricted to sources with at least OVERPERFORMING_SOURCE_MIN_PUBLICATIONS (10) publications in that quartile. This is the one sub-question a tier-average chart structurally cannot answer — "expected for that tier" is itself a per-source, not per-tier, computation — and the floor stops one highly-cited paper from manufacturing the appearance of an outlier.用的数据：get_overperforming_sources() 输出的sources 表格——每一行是一个(分位, Scopus期刊来源)组合，带publications、 mean_fwci两列，外加一个gap列 = 该来源自己的mean_fwci 减去它自己所在分位的mean_fwci，并限定该来源在该分位内至少有 OVERPERFORMING_SOURCE_MIN_PUBLICATIONS（10）篇发文量。这是一个按档位平均的 图表在结构上根本回答不了的问题——"超出该档位预期"本身就是一个按来源、而不是按档位算的 量——发文量下限则避免一篇高被引论文就把一个来源伪装成"表现突出"。

Research Field / Faculty Analysis研究学科/院系分析

app/pages/3_Field_Analysis.py — which fields are strongest, improving, or under-performing. app/pages/3_Field_Analysis.py —— 哪些学科最强、在进步、或表现不足。

Closed since 2026-09-042026-09-04之后补上的 "Are particular publication strategies more successful in some fields than others?" was previously left unanswered — "strategy" has no client-confirmed definition. It's now answered via the closest concrete, computable stand-in this dataset supports (collaboration approach: domestic/single-institution vs. domestic/multi-institution vs. international), crossed with field. "某些发表策略是否在特定学科更有效？"之前一直没有回答——"策略"这个词客户 没有给出确定的定义。现在用这个数据集能支持的、最接近的一个可计算的替代变量来回答 （合作方式：国内单机构 / 国内多机构 / 国际合作），并按学科交叉分析。

Sub-question coverage子问题覆盖情况

✓ Which fields produce the highest publication volume?哪些学科的发文量最高？ Data: the field_summary table from field_summary() — one row per field, columns publications, mean_fwci, q1_share, top_decile_share, uncited_share. It's sortable on its publications column directly — no separate chart needed when a ranked table already answers "which is highest" precisely, to the exact count.用的数据：field_summary() 生成的field_summary 表格——每个学科一行，列有publications、mean_fwci、 q1_share、top_decile_share、uncited_share。 可以直接按 publications 列排序——当一张可排序的表格已经能 精确到具体数字地回答"哪个最高"时，不需要另外画一张图。
✓ Which fields achieve the strongest impact?哪些学科的影响力最强？ Same field_summary table, mean_fwci column — kept in one table with publications and q1_share deliberately, so a reader can immediately see whether a field's strength is broad (high on all three columns) or narrow (high on one, unremarkable on the others).同一张field_summary表的 mean_fwci 列——特意跟 publications、q1_share放在同一张表里， 这样读者能立刻看出一个学科的优势是全面的（三列都高）还是单一的（只有一列高）。
✓ Which fields have the highest Q1 publication share?哪些学科的Q1发表份额最高？ Third sortable column (q1_share) of the same field_summary table — also broken out as its own dedicated bar chart (the q1_field_df table, same column, sorted) further down the page for anyone who wants the ranking without the rest of the table.同一张field_summary表的第三个可排序列（q1_share）—— 页面下方还专门做了一张独立的条形图（q1_field_df表格，同一列，已排序）， 方便只想看这一项排名、不想看整张表的人。
✓ Which fields have high volume but relatively low citation impact?哪些学科发文量高但影响力偏低？ Data: the quadrant table from volume_vs_impact_quadrant(field_summary) — the same field_summary table's publications (x) plotted against mean_fwci (y), each field classified into one of 4 bands by whether it's above/below the median of each column across all fields. This combination — and its mirror image below — reads off as a labelled quadrant, rather than requiring the reader to sort two separate columns and cross-reference them by hand.用的数据：volume_vs_impact_quadrant(field_summary) 生成的 quadrant表格——还是field_summary表的publications列 （x轴）对mean_fwci列（y轴），按每一列在全部学科里的中位数以上/以下， 把每个学科分进4个象限之一。这种组合——以及下面它的镜像组合——直接对应到一个有标签的象限， 不用读者自己排序两列数字再手动交叉核对。
✓ Which fields have relatively low volume but very strong citation impact?哪些学科发文量低但影响力很强？ Same quadrant table, the opposite corner — points where publications is below-median and mean_fwci is above-median get the "low volume, high impact" label directly from volume_vs_impact_quadrant(), visible in that region rather than needing to be inferred.同一张quadrant表格，对角的那个象限——publications低于 中位数、mean_fwci高于中位数的点，会被volume_vs_impact_quadrant() 直接标成"低产出量、高影响力"，落在这个区域里的点直接就是答案，不需要再推断。
✓ Which areas are improving most rapidly, and which are declining?哪些领域进步最快，哪些在退步？ Data: the growth_df table from field_growth() — one row per field, a growth column (period_growth: last-year count minus first-year count, over first-year count, within MAIN_YEAR_RANGE), sorted descending for the bar, plus explicit "most improved" / "most declined" text call-outs computed live off the same column — the sentence form exists specifically so the answer doesn't require reading bar heights off a chart with 10+ fields.用的数据：field_growth() 生成的growth_df表格—— 每个学科一行，growth列（period_growth：MAIN_YEAR_RANGE 范围内，末年发文量减首年发文量、再除以首年发文量），降序排列画柱状图，加上根据同一列 实时算出的"进步最快"/"退步最快"文字总结——特意做成一句话的形式，这样答案就不用从一张有 10多个学科的图上去读柱子高度。
✓ Which fields produce the highest proportion of overperforming (top-decile) publications?哪些学科的高被引（前10%）论文占比最高？ The field_summary table's top_decile_share column — a rate, deliberately kept distinct from the next question's count, since a small field can have a high rate on very few papers.field_summary表的 top_decile_share 列——这是一个比例， 特意跟下一个问题的"数量"区分开，因为一个小学科可能只凭很少几篇论文就拿到很高的比例。
✓ Which fields produce the largest number of highly cited publications?哪些学科的高被引论文绝对数量最多？ Data: the highly_cited_df table — field_summary with an added highly_cited_count column computed as publications × top_decile_share, rounded. top_decile_share alone is a rate; multiplying it back out by each field's publication count gives an actual number, specifically so a small field's high rate isn't mistaken for a large field's large count of highly-cited papers — the two questions can and do have different answers.用的数据：highly_cited_df表格——在field_summary基础上 加了一列highly_cited_count，算法是publications × top_decile_share 再四舍五入。top_decile_share本身是个比例；这张图把它乘回各学科的发文量， 算出实际的数字，就是为了避免把"小学科的高比例"误当成"大学科的高被引论文绝对数量多"—— 这两个问题的答案确实可能不一样。
~ Which fields appear to represent existing strengths, and where is the greatest potential for improvement?哪些学科代表现有优势，哪些最有提升潜力？ "Strengths / potential" links to the same quadrant table — its four quadrant labels are the closest single-table answer (high-publications/high-mean_fwci reads as an existing strength, low-publications/high-mean_fwci as potential upside) — but the full answer is a synthesis with the field_summary.q1_share and growth_df.growth columns too, not that table alone."优势/潜力"链接到同一张quadrant表格——它的四个象限标签是最接近一个 现成答案的单张表（高publications+高mean_fwci读作现有优势， 低publications+高mean_fwci读作潜力）——但完整的答案是 结合field_summary的q1_share列和growth_df的 growth列一起做的综合判断，不是单靠那一张表就够。
✓ Are particular publication strategies more successful in some fields than others?某些发表策略是否在特定学科更有效？ Data: the approach_df table from collaboration_approach_by_field() — field × collaboration_approach (International / Domestic multi-institution / Domestic single-institution, from add_collaboration_approach()'s is_international and is_multi_institution flags) as the two grouping columns, with publications and mean_fwci per combination. "Publication strategy" has no client-confirmed definition, so this uses the closest concrete stand-in the dataset supports, with the per-field international-advantage margin (intl_gap, a pivoted difference of mean_fwci columns) computed and stated explicitly underneath so "more successful in some fields than others" has an actual number, not just a chart to eyeball.用的数据：collaboration_approach_by_field() 生成的 approach_df表格——学科×collaboration_approach（国际合作/国内 多机构/国内单机构，来自add_collaboration_approach()基于 is_international和is_multi_institution两个标记算出的分类） 两个分组维度，每种组合一行publications和mean_fwci。"发表策略" 客户没有给出确定的定义，所以用这个数据集能支持的最接近的一个具体替代变量，图表下方还 明确算出了每个学科的国际合作优势幅度（intl_gap，把mean_fwci列 透视后相减得到），这样"在某些学科更有效"这句话就有一个具体数字支撑，不只是让读者自己 看图估计。

International Collaboration Analysis国际合作分析

app/pages/4_International_Collaboration.py — are internationally co-authored papers cited more frequently. app/pages/4_International_Collaboration.py —— 国际合著的论文是否被引用得更多。

Sub-question coverage子问题覆盖情况

✓ Do internationally collaborative publications receive higher citation impact?国际合作的论文是否有更高的引用影响力？ Data: the status table from impact_by_collaboration_status() — two rows (International/Domestic, split on is_international), columns publications, mean_fwci, q1_share, top_decile_share, uncited_share. "增加与特定高绩效机构的合作"和"将部分发表转向有潜力的期刊"这两条，最初都被 判定为需要平台没有的数据。仔细检查之后发现这个判断是错的：Institutions 和 Scopus Source title 这两列本身就带着所需的全部信息，只要按平台其它 地方处理多值字段的同一套方法拆开、按合作机构/按期刊来源分别匹配就行。

Sub-question coverage子问题覆盖情况

✓ Increase Q1 publication share by N percentage points.将Q1发表份额提高N个百分点。 Data: the table from scenario_table(dedup, delta_pp) — one row per scenario, columns current_share, projected_share, mean_when_true, mean_when_false, current_mean_metric, projected_mean_metric. The is_q1 row computes current vs. projected mean FWCI if Q1 share shifted by delta_pp, using mean_when_true minus mean_when_false (the actual historical Q1-vs-non-Q1 FWCI gap in this dataset) as the per-point uplift, rather than an assumed number.用的数据：scenario_table(dedup, delta_pp) 输出的table—— 每个情景一行，列有current_share、projected_share、 mean_when_true、mean_when_false、 current_mean_metric、projected_mean_metric。is_q1 那一行算的正是：如果Q1份额按delta_pp变化，现状vs预测的平均FWCI是多少， 每一个百分点的提升幅度用的是mean_when_true减mean_when_false （这份数据里实际的Q1对非Q1的FWCI差距），不是一个假设出来的数字。
✓ Increase international collaboration by N percentage points.将国际合作提高N个百分点。 Same table, the is_international row — mean_when_true minus mean_when_false here is the historical international-vs-domestic gap in place of the Q1 gap.同一张table，is_international那一行——这里的 mean_when_true减mean_when_false是国际合作对国内合作的历史 差距，而不是Q1的差距。
✓ Increase open-access publication where evidence suggests a benefit.在有证据显示有益的地方提高开放获取比例。 Same table, the is_open_access row, flagged with a PROVISIONAL caption specifically because how open-access null values are treated (OPEN_ACCESS_NULL_MEANS_NOT_OA) is a client-unconfirmed assumption this scenario inherits directly.同一张table，is_open_access那一行，特意标注了 PROVISIONAL说明——因为开放获取字段空值该怎么处理 （OPEN_ACCESS_NULL_MEANS_NOT_OA）是一个客户还没确认的假设，这个情景直接 继承了这个假设。
~ Reduce the proportion of low-impact publications.降低低影响力论文的比例。 Same table, the is_uncited row (framed as "↓ uncited share") — projected via Citations == 0, the closest binary flag this dataset actually has. "Low-impact" itself is undefined by the client and is not the same claim as "uncited": a publication can have low-but-nonzero impact and this row wouldn't move it.同一张table，is_uncited那一行（标成"↓零引用份额"）—— 用Citations == 0来做预测，这是数据集里现有最接近的二元标记。"低影响力"本身 客户没有给出定义，跟"零引用"不是一回事：一篇论文可能影响力低但不为零，这一行 对它是不起作用的。
→ Improve publication performance within selected research areas.提升特定研究领域的发表表现。 The Go8 Benchmarking page's gap_df.gap column already identifies exactly where Sydney trails, by field — that's the "selected research areas" input a scenario like this would need, but it isn't turned into its own row in this page's table; doing so would mean picking which fields' publications get reassigned a target FWCI, a scope decision this page doesn't make on its own.Go8 Benchmarking页面的gap_df.gap列已经按学科明确指出了悉尼落后在 哪里——这正是这类情景需要的"特定研究领域"输入，但这里没有把它转成这个页面 table里独立的一行；要做到这一点，需要决定挑选哪些学科的论文去赋予一个 目标FWCI，这是一个这个页面自己无法单方面决定的范围界定问题。
✓ Increase collaboration with selected high-performing institutions.增加与特定高绩效机构的合作。 Data: the partner_perf table from get_institution_partner_performance() — one row per partner institution, columns publications, mean_fwci, and gap = that partner's mean_fwci minus Sydney's own overall mean, floored at HIGH_PERFORMING_PARTNER_MIN_PUBLICATIONS (10) co-authored publications. The Institutions column (pipe-delimited co-author affiliations) turned out to support the exact same per-partner computation already built for journals — the volume floor stops a single big collaboration from faking a partner's ranking. That converts the question into the same shape as the other scenarios: shift some share of publications toward the group already flagged as above-average.用的数据：get_institution_partner_performance() 生成的 partner_perf表格——每个合作机构一行，列有publications、 mean_fwci，外加一个gap列 = 该机构的mean_fwci减去 悉尼自己的整体平均，限定至少HIGH_PERFORMING_PARTNER_MIN_PUBLICATIONS （10）篇合著发文量。Institutions列（用竖线分隔的合著机构名单）实际上支持 跟期刊那边一模一样的按机构算法——发文量下限避免一次大型合作就伪造出某个机构的排名。 这样一来，这个问题就变成了和其它情景同样的形状：把一部分发表转向已经被标记为高于 平均水平的那一组合作机构。
✓ Shift some publications toward journals identified as strong opportunities.将部分发表转向被识别为"有潜力"的期刊。 Wires journal_tier.source_performance_flag()'s per-publication boolean straight into the scenario table as an is_source_overperforming row (merged in via dedup.assign() in get_scenario_table(), before calling scenario_table()), scoped to the ~74% of publications whose source has enough volume in its tier to carry a defined flag — turning the Journal Tier page's sources shortlist from a static table into an actual FWCI projection, the same mechanism as the other rows above.把journal_tier.source_performance_flag()算出的按论文一行的布尔标记， 直接接入情景table，作为is_source_overperforming这一行 （在get_scenario_table()里通过dedup.assign()合并进去， 再调用scenario_table()），范围限定在约74%的、期刊来源在其所在档位内有 足够发文量、能定义出标记的那部分发表——把Journal Tier页面sources表格那份 静态候选名单，变成一个实际的FWCI预测，用的是跟上面其它行一样的机制。
A caveat that applies to both new scenarios两个新情景共同的一条提醒 Both the institution-partner and journal-source over-performer lists skew toward large multi-author, multi-site collaborations (clinical trials, big journals with huge volume) — plausibly genuine high-value partners, but also exactly the pattern a handful of huge collaborations would produce even without the partner itself adding anything beyond being part of them. Treat both as shortlists to investigate, not verdicts — stated explicitly on the page itself, not just here. 机构合作和期刊来源这两份"跑赢"名单都偏向大型多作者、多地点的合作项目 （临床试验、发文量巨大的大期刊）——这些可能确实是真正有价值的合作对象，但也正好是 少数几个超大型合作项目会产生的模式，哪怕这个合作机构本身并没有在"参与这个大项目"之外 带来额外的价值。两份名单都应该当成需要进一步调查的候选清单，不是定论——这一点在 页面本身就已经明确写出来了，不只是在这份文档里提。
