## Attendance and Meetings

We agree to attend all group meetings punctually, as well as any additional scheduled meetings where necessary.

Our regular meetings are:

- Wednesday, 15:00–17:00
- Friday, 21:30–22:20

## Record Keeping

The Manager is responsible for promptly maintaining the following information in the repository Wiki or documentation area:

- Project brief and background material, so the team shares the same understanding of client needs.
- Minutes for both weekly meetings, including decisions and action items with named owners.
- A progress report showing completed work and outstanding tasks for each sub-team.

### Previous Gaps

Week 1 was mostly informal. The project choice and initial role discussion occurred in chat and were not formally documented.

## Participation

We undertake to participate fully and work as a team. This means that every member will:

- Attend both weekly meetings.
- Bring either a completed task or a clearly stated blocker.
- Reply in the team communication channel within 24 hours.
- Review at least one teammate's Pull Request each week.

To maintain participation and commitment:

- Every task must have a named owner.
- Workload is reviewed at each meeting.
- Additional members are assigned if a task becomes heavier than expected.
- Each member reports progress as completed, in progress, or blocked at both weekly meetings.

## Conflict Resolution and Skill Development

We will discuss problems respectfully, listen to all perspectives, and negotiate a solution.

The team identified differences in technical experience, particularly in Python, Git, and data analysis. To address this fairly, we agree to:

- Use peer teaching and pairing so knowledge is transferred through project work.
- Recognise skill development as a legitimate part of allocated workload for members who are still building required capabilities.
- Review the balance between learning and delivery as skills develop.

## Demo Rehearsal

Before each group demonstration, the team will conduct an online rehearsal:

- **When:** Friday, 21:30–22:20, during the week before the demo
- **Where:** Online

## Fair Task Allocation

We will distribute work fairly and equitably. Task allocation will consider:

- The estimated effort and complexity of each task.
- Each member's current skills and opportunities for learning.
- The need to balance workload across the team.
- Progress and blockers identified during weekly meetings.

## Agreement

All team members agree to follow this contract and revisit it when the project's needs change.
